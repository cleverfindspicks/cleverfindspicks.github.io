# Pinterest creative gate

Every newly approved product needs a dedicated vertical Pinterest image before publication.

- Minimum height-to-width ratio: 1.4 (2:3 is preferred).
- Use the original AliExpress product image, or a real crop/cut-out from it, whenever possible.
- The product itself must remain factually identical: do not change its shape, dimensions, colour, piece count, functions or included accessories, and do not imply a feature that the listing does not verify.
- Generated elements may be limited to the background, typography, shadows, layout and decorative treatment around the real product.
- Record the source product image used and confirm visual fidelity during review.
- If a faithful pin cannot be made from the available listing evidence, reject the creative and continue with the next qualified candidate instead of publishing a misleading image.
- Make the problem and solution understandable within a few seconds.
- Use a short, specific benefit rather than clickbait, urgency or exaggerated claims.
- Keep text readable on mobile and preserve enough empty space around it.
- Do not reuse the square AliExpress listing image as the final pin.
- Record `path`, `width`, `height` and `reviewedNonClickbait: true` in the candidate record.

The pre-publication check rejects a new candidate when this evidence is missing.
